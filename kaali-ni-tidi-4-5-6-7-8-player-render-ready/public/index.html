How a round works

  1. Starting after the dealer, players bid clockwise or pass. A new bid must be higher by the mode's bid increment.
  2. The highest bidder chooses Hukum and calls the required number of exact hidden-partner cards. In two-deck modes, Deck 1 / Deck 2 identifies the physical copy.
  3. Each called partner card must be held by a different player. Partners remain secret until their called card is played.
  4. The highest bidder leads the first trick. Play continues clockwise.
  5. You must follow the lead suit when possible. If void, you may play Hukum or discard another suit.
  6. 3♠ is the absolute highest card when legally played. Otherwise highest Hukum wins; with no Hukum, highest card of the lead suit wins.
  7. If two identical physical-value cards from different decks appear in the same trick, the one played first ranks higher. If both 3♠ cards appear, the first 3♠ played wins.
  8. Trick points belong to the trick winner. At round end, the bidder side must reach or exceed the winning bid.
